<h2 class = "header-title-alt"> Design </h2>

<p> 
    The main concept is a blank/dead tree, and then people can add in any sort of text-to-speech message, a reactionary image/gif, or a drawing. Over time, their messages would be counted as a leaf/message, and it would grow as more people add or type stuff into it.
</p>
