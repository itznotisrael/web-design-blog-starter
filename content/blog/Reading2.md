<h2 class = "header-title-alt"> The Age of Surveillance Capitalism - Shoshana Zuboff </h2>

<p> Zuboff argues that we are living in a new economic system called surveillance capitalism, where companies collect vast amounts of personal data from our online behavior and turn it into profit. Instead of just providing services, tech companies extract behavioral data (searches, clicks, locations, likes) and use it to predict and even influence—our future actions. </p>

<h2 class = "header-title-alt"> “Facebook Got Rid of Racial Ad Categories. Or Did It?” - The Markup </h2>

<p>  This investigation examines whether Facebook truly eliminated racial ad targeting categories after public criticism and legal pressure.

Although Facebook officially removed explicit racial targeting options like “African-American multicultural affinity,” The Markup’s Citizen Browser project found that advertisers could still effectively target racial groups using indirect “proxy” categories.  </p>
