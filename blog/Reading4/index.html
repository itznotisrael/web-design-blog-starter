Reading IV

  • 14 April 2026

Another Network is Possible - April Glaser

This article tells the story of Indymedia, an early internet-based activist media network that emerged during the 1999 Seattle WTO protests. It was created so activists could control their own narratives instead of relying on corporate media like CNN.

Architecting Emergence - Zhenzhen Qi & Yang Wang

This article explores how systems, technology, and collaboration shape creativity and society, focusing on the concept of emergence—when complex outcomes arise from simple interactions.

The woman who taught internet strangers to actually care for one another - Claire L. Evans

This article highlights 'Stacy Horn', founder of one of the earliest online communities, Echo (a BBS), and contrasts early internet culture with today’s social media.

Reflection

My personal takeaway is that the internet isn't a fixed system, it’s a human creation that reflects our values. The articles shows it reflects profit, scale, and control. However, it could reflect care, creativity, and community.
