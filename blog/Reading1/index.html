How to See Invisible Infrastructure - Ingrid Burrington

This piece argues that the systems powering modern life—like the internet and data networks—are deliberately or structurally invisible to most people. Infrastructure fades into the background when it works smoothly, becoming noticeable only when it breaks.

Reflection

What stands out most is how convincing the illusion of the “cloud” is. Before reading this kind of material, it’s easy to think of the internet as something abstract—almost like air. But reading the text force you to confront the reality that every message, video, or search is traveling through very specific routes, owned by specific actors, in specific places.
